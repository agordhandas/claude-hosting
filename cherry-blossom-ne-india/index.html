waterfallcaveroot bridge
▾

Assumptions & Notes

  • Gateway city: Guwahati (LGBI Airport) assumed as the arrival and departure point, as it's the nearest major airport to all three regions.
  • Route order: Kaziranga first (eastward from Guwahati), then north to Tawang, then south to Meghalaya. This minimises backtracking and saves Shillong's Cherry Blossom Festival for the last leg when it's most likely in full bloom.
  • Tawang leg (5 days): Bomdila is used as an overnight halt in each direction because the Tezpur-to-Tawang drive (~12 hrs) is too long for a single day, especially on mountain roads.
  • Bumla Pass army permit: Assumed to be pre-arranged by the tour operator. Civilian visits require Indian Army permission, sometimes cancelled due to weather or security. BSNL is the only network at Bumla.
  • Inner Line Permit (ILP): Required for Indian citizens entering Arunachal Pradesh. Assumed arranged by the operator in advance.
  • Mawlynnong & Living Root Bridge are clubbed with the Cherrapunji day. In reality this is a packed day — the operator may split across two days or adjust based on group pace.
  • Dawki on departure day: Assumes evening/late-afternoon flights from Guwahati. For early flights, Dawki may be moved to Day 11 instead.
  • Drive times are best-case estimates for November conditions. Mountain roads to Tawang can add 1–2 hours depending on weather, convoys, and road work.
  • Cherry Blossom Festival dates are typically mid-to-late November, matching this tour's Shillong leg (Nov 23–25). Exact festival dates are announced by the Meghalaya government closer to the event.
  • Urbania coaches are used on plains and highways. For Tawang mountain roads, smaller vehicles (Innova/Bolero) may be substituted for safety and manoeuvrability.
  • Meals: "Rajwadi" refers to a dedicated Gujarati/Rajasthani-style travel kitchen, common for Gujarat-based tour operators catering to vegetarian groups.
  • November weather: Expect 5–15°C in Shillong, sub-zero at Bumla Pass. Warm layers and rain gear recommended.
