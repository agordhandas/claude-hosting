The Methodology
Behind the Plugins

Every plugin runs on the same four questions. Worth studying even if you never use Claude for Legal. The tools will change. This framework won't.
1
Who are you?
Practice Profile. Your firm's playbooks, risk calibration, escalation chains, house style. The cold-start interview that teaches the system how your firm operates.
2
How do you do it?
Skills. The specific task workflows: review this contract against this playbook, draft this motion in this structure, run this checklist against these facts.
3
When do you do it?
Agents. Multi-step coordination. Break a complex engagement into phases, run them in sequence or parallel, hand off between steps.
4
Where is the data?
Connectors. iManage, Westlaw, DocuSign, NetDocuments, LexisNexis, Microsoft 365. The system needs to read from and write to where lawyers already work.
